# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Muscu App is for beginners and intermediates training in a gym or at home, mostly on mobile, who want to move fast during a workout session without juggling multiple tools.

## Product Purpose

Help the user run a full workout session with the least friction possible: open the session, know the next exercise, start the rest timer, log weight and reps, and recover a simple history of progress.

Success means the app disappears into the workout, stays readable under fatigue, and makes session tracking feel automatic rather than administrative.

## Brand Personality

Direct, intense, practical.

The tone should feel like a serious training tool, not a social network, not a coaching marketplace, and not an encyclopedic content site.

## Anti-references

- Social fitness platforms with noisy feeds and community-first layouts.
- Overdesigned dashboard UIs that turn a workout into a reporting exercise.
- Decorative, playful, or overly editorial interfaces that slow down the task.
- Anything that makes the user hunt for the timer, the next set, or the save action.

## Design Principles

- One primary action per screen.
- The current session must always be obvious.
- Logging a set should feel faster than counting it mentally.
- Exercise cues should be short and immediately usable in the gym.
- Local-first persistence is part of the product promise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Mobile-first responsive UI.
- Strong contrast for low-light gym use and fatigue.
- Clear touch targets and keyboard support.
- Reduced motion should be respected.
- Avoid relying on color alone to communicate state.
